# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Disagreement about code and product decisions
is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, and bad-faith engagement
are not.

Maintainers may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions and participation that violate
these standards. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's private
vulnerability-reporting form with a title beginning `Conduct report:`. Do not publish
sensitive conduct reports in issues or discussions.
